
# 📊 Mobile Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## 🚀 Project Overview

The **Mobile Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze mobile phone sales performance across different cities, brands, payment methods, and time periods.

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales trends, customer behavior, transaction patterns, and product performance, enabling stakeholders to make data-driven business decisions.


## 🎯 Objectives

* Monitor overall sales performance.
* Track sales trends across months.
* Analyze city-wise sales distribution.
* Compare brand performance.
* Evaluate customer ratings.
* Understand payment method preferences.
* Identify top-performing mobile models.

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides:

* 💰 Total Sales
* 📦 Total Quantity Sold
* 💳 Total Transactions
* 📊 Average Sales

### 🗺️ Geographic Analysis

**Total Sales by City**

* Interactive map visualization
* City-wise sales comparison
* Regional performance insight

### 📅 Time-Based Analysis

**Total Quantity by Month**

* Monthly sales trend analysis
* Seasonal demand identification
* Growth and decline tracking

### ⭐ Customer Satisfaction Analysis

**Customer Ratings**

* Rating distribution visualization
* Customer feedback trends
* Satisfaction measurement

### 💳 Payment Method Analysis

**Transactions by Payment Method**

Comparison of:

* UPI
* Debit Card
* Credit Card
* Cash

Helps identify preferred payment options.

### 📱 Product Performance Analysis

**Total Sales by Mobile Model**

* Best-selling mobile models
* Product-wise revenue contribution
* Model performance comparison

### 📆 Sales by Day Analysis

**Total Sales by Day Name**

* Weekday performance tracking
* Peak sales day identification
* Customer purchasing behavior analysis
